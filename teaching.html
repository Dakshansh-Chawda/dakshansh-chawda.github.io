  • Computational Physics
  • Theoretical Physics Seminars
  • Advanced Topics in Particle Physics

Engagement

Science Communication

Communicating cutting-edge physics research to broader audiences through various platforms.

Communication Activities:

  • Medium articles on physics and machine learning
  • Scientific writing and blog posts
  • Seminar presentations to academic audiences
  • Discussion of research on social media
  • Contributions to scientific forums and Q&A platforms

Community

Workshops & Tutorials

Organizing and leading workshops to share technical knowledge with the physics community.

Workshop Topics:

  • Machine Learning in Physics
  • Python for Scientific Computing
  • Introduction to Neural Networks
  • Computational Methods in QCD
  • Data Analysis and Visualization

Collaboration

Mentoring & Collaboration

Supporting student and early-career physicist development through mentoring relationships.

Mentoring Activities:

  • Guiding student research projects
  • Career development discussions
  • Research methodology and best practices
  • Publication and presentation guidance
  • Technical skill development

Philosophy: Each person's journey through physics is unique. Effective mentoring recognizes individual strengths and learning styles while providing the support needed for success.

Outreach

Public Engagement

Connecting with the general public and school communities to inspire future physicists.

Outreach Activities:

  • Physics demonstrations and experiments
  • High school and university guest lectures
  • Science fair and exhibition participation
  • Public seminar series
  • Social media science education
