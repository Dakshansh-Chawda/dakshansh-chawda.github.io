Finding Links

Where to Get Publication URLs

For arXiv papers:

  • Go to arxiv.org
  • Search for your paper
  • Copy the arxiv ID (format: XXXX.XXXXX)
  • Use URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/XXXX.XXXXX

For journal articles:

  • Find DOI on the journal website
  • Use direct link or DOI resolver
  • Example: https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP12(2025)XXX
